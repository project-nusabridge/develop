# PROJECT VISION

**Project Name:** NusaBridge

**Version:** 1.0

**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# Vision

Become the most trusted business platform helping Chinese companies successfully expand into Indonesia.

NusaBridge aims to reduce the complexity, risk and uncertainty of entering the Indonesian market by combining local execution capability, professional consulting and strong China supply chain resources.

---

# Mission

Help Chinese businesses enter Indonesia faster, safer and more efficiently.

We provide practical, execution-oriented business services instead of only consulting.

---

# Positioning

NusaBridge is a China–Indonesia Business Growth Partner.

We are not:

- A traditional trading company
- A simple registration agency
- A freight forwarder
- A sourcing agent

We are a long-term business partner helping companies establish and grow their business in Indonesia.
